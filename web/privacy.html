Your play. Your privacy.

Last updated: September 16, 2026.

What this arcade stores

No account is needed. Favorites, recently played games, sound settings, and high scores stay in your browser or Android app storage. The arcade does not upload your scores or collect names, email addresses, contacts, microphone recordings, or precise location.

Advertising

This preview uses Google test ads in the Android app. Website advertising is disabled. Test ads do not earn revenue. If live advertising is enabled in a later release, Google may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to provide ads and measure performance. The app requests consent where required before loading live ads.

When available, use “Ad privacy choices” in the app or website to change advertising consent. Games remain free when ads are unavailable. See Google’s advertising privacy information.

Local data

You can delete local favorites and scores by clearing this website’s storage in your browser or clearing the Android app’s data. Removing the app also removes its local data. Your browser and hosting provider may retain ordinary security and access logs.

Games and children

The included games are general-audience arcade games. New games can have their own disclosures and external services. Games marked for children suppress app advertising. The operator must review audience declarations and privacy disclosures before adding child-directed content or activating live ads.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning information you submitted separately, contact the project owner through the repository. Do not post personal information in public issues.
